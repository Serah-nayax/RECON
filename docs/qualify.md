# Qualification and ICPs

The Lead Qualifier scores each lead against the **active ICP**. Before a batch starts, RECON asks which ICP to use.

## Running a batch

- **From Maps:** *Qualify All* on a search.
- **From a list:** upload a CSV or Excel file (a name, ideally a website), or paste a list.

There is no limit on the number of leads. A batch can be stopped and retried, and resumes on its own after a restart. Past batches are listed under the form, on the same page: open one to review its leads and download them.

## How a lead is scored

1. RECON reads the website (home and the most useful pages) and the Maps info.
2. The AI (Claude Haiku) checks which ICP signals the lead meets, each backed by a quote from the site.
3. RECON computes the score from these facts: **QUALIFY** (60+), **BORDERLINE** (40–59), **DISQUALIFY** (under 40).

Verdicts are stable: each lead gets 2 opinions, plus a 3rd if they disagree. Only a lead that is clearly the ICP's kind of venue can reach QUALIFY.

For leads worth contacting, the AI also writes sales notes: pitch angle, opening hook, next action.

## Export

The Excel file holds score, verdict, venue type, estimated machines, payment system, competitor, contacts and sales notes.

Leads that are already Tigapo locations get the same **Tigapo** badge as on Maps Search, and the Excel has a Tigapo column. Leads from Maps are matched by position; leads from a list by city, name and street address.

## ICPs

An ICP describes the customers you want: who they are, what makes a good lead, what rules one out. One ICP is active at a time for the whole team. It is used by the Qualifier and suggests its search terms on the Maps page. Each ICP's search terms can be changed on the ICP page.

## Cost

About $2 per 1,000 leads. The Qualify page shows the AI cost of the batch and of the month.
